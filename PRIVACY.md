# DWIRC Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 August 2026

DWIRC is an open-source IRC client maintained by DarkWorld Network.

## Information stored

DWIRC does not include advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting
SDKs. DarkWorld Network does not automatically receive usage statistics from
the app.

DWIRC may store IRC configurations, nicknames, credentials, preferences,
optional logs, encryption keys and transferred files locally on the user's
device.

## Network communications

DWIRC communicates with IRC servers configured or selected by the user. Its
bundled profile connects to `irc.darkworld.network`.

Optional features may contact linked media providers, file-hosting services,
proxy servers or websites used by the user. Third-party IRC networks and
services have their own privacy practices.

## Permissions

Depending on the Android version and enabled features, DWIRC may request
permissions for notifications, network access, foreground connection services,
files, downloads or local-network DCC communication.

## Data sharing

DWIRC does not sell personal information or send user data to DarkWorld Network
for advertising or analytics.

Messages and account information necessarily pass through the selected IRC
server. End-to-end encryption applies only when a compatible secure-chat
feature is enabled.

## Retention and deletion

App data remains on the device until deleted in DWIRC, through Android settings
or by uninstalling the app. IRC servers and bouncers may retain information
under their own policies.

## Children

DWIRC is a general-purpose communications client and is not specifically
directed to children.

## Changes

Material changes will be published in the DWIRC repository and, when
applicable, on the DarkWorld website.
